# Bank Document Workspace

**An AI-assisted workspace for reviewing Armenian-language lending documents.**

A lending file brings together bank decisions, identity documents, statements,
consents and supporting evidence. The difficult part is connecting them: who each
document belongs to, which requirement it supports, what is missing, and which
information needs a person's attention.

Bank Document Workspace brings that process into one place.

## What the product does

- **Organizes a case:** collect a bank decision and supporting documents in a
  single workspace, with batch and folder uploads.
- **Reads and connects evidence:** extract information, identify relevant people
  and propose document-to-person connections with source references.
- **Makes review actionable:** show missing documents, uncertain matches and
  conflicting information; let an employee correct and confirm the relevant data.
- **Prepares data for AI:** mask personal information while preserving supported
  financial information and relationships, with review of the exact output.
- **Supports questions about the file:** use approved masked evidence for an AI
  conversation, then restore recognized references locally for the employee.

## A focused experience

The private demonstration uses four areas: **Overview, Documents, Privacy review,
and AI assistant**. Detailed source evidence remains available when needed.

The intended result is a clear answer to three practical questions:

1. What do we have?
2. What needs attention?
3. What can we establish from the available evidence?

## Product principles

**Human accountability.** AI assists review. It does not approve loans or replace
an employee's assessment of the evidence.

**Traceable corrections.** Readings, corrections, source references and review
history remain distinguishable.

**Useful privacy controls.** Masking should protect identifying information while
retaining the context needed for useful analysis. Supported exports are reviewed
before use by the AI assistant.

**Honest uncertainty.** Missing, partial, conflicting and unsupported information
must remain visible rather than becoming a confident answer.

## Current stage

Working local prototype with a separate private demonstration environment and
synthetic sample data. Document support and quality vary by format and layout.
Further validation and operational work are required before production use.
